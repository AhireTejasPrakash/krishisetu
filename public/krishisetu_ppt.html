⚠️ The Problem

India's Farmers are Flying Blind

📉
Wrong Crop Selection
Farmers choose crops based on tradition, not soil data — leading to low yield and loss.
💸
Middlemen Exploitation
Lack of real-time mandi prices forces farmers to sell below market value.
🌦️
No Weather Alerts
No timely irrigation or pest warnings cause massive crop damage every season.
📜
Schemes Unreachable
Most farmers are unaware of PM-KISAN, PMFBY and other government subsidies they qualify for.
✅ Our Solution

One App. Every Farm Need.

KrishiSetu bridges the gap between technology and the Indian farmer.

💰
Live Mandi Prices
Real-time crop prices from backend database, updated daily with market fluctuation

Real-Time Weather + Smart Alerts

🏛️
Government Schemes Portal
PM-KISAN, PMFBY, KCC — real eligibility info, helplines, and direct links to official portals
