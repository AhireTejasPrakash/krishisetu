# 🌾 KrishiSetu

### AI-Based Market-Linked Crop Planning, Rural Advisory & Agricultural Operations Platform

KrishiSetu is a smart agriculture platform designed to help farmers make better farming decisions using **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, weather information, market prices, and agricultural advisory**.

The platform connects the complete farming journey — from **crop selection and planning to weather monitoring, farm operations, market analysis, and profit estimation**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers often make crop decisions based on traditional practices or limited information.

Some of the major problems are:

- Choosing crops without considering soil conditions.
- Difficulty understanding which crop may be more suitable for the current season.
- Changing market prices and lack of transparent price information.
- Unpredictable rainfall, temperature, and humidity.
- Lack of timely agricultural advisory.
- Difficulty finding and understanding government agricultural schemes.
- Different agricultural services being available on separate platforms.

KrishiSetu aims to bring these important services together in one easy-to-use platform.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

KrishiSetu provides a centralized digital platform where farmers can:

🌱 Get AI-based crop recommendations  
📊 Check agricultural market prices  
🌦️ Receive weather information and alerts  
🚜 Manage farming operations and tasks  
💰 Estimate expected investment and profit  
🛡️ Receive crop and weather-based advisory  
🏛️ Find information about government schemes  
🌐 Use the platform in English, Hindi, and Marathi  

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 🌱 1. AI Crop Recommendation

The system recommends suitable crops based on important soil and weather parameters.

### Input Parameters

- Soil Nitrogen (N)
- Soil Phosphorus (P)
- Soil Potassium (K)
- Soil pH
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Rainfall

The Machine Learning model analyzes these parameters and provides suitable crop recommendations.
